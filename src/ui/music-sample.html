Delve

Dwarven chant · made live in your browser, no recordings

The moods

Tap one to start. Tap another to change mood; the game would switch on its own.

Calls

Short cues that play over the music when something happens.

What you're hearing

  • Plainchant: one melody sung by the whole choir together, in the old Dorian mode, in free rhythm, with long runs of notes on the last syllable of each line.
  • Organum: on some lines a second choir sings the same words a fifth below, as medieval monks did.
  • Dwarvish: invented words with hard k, d and g, rolled rs and humming ms.
  • The Viking edge: a held drone with a throat-singing growl an octave beneath it, frame drums, war horns, and shouts in battle.
  • The hall: a long stone echo around everything.

These voices are synthesized: expect a choir of stone, not a recording. No sound on an iPhone? Turn the volume up, and if it's still silent, flip the ring/silent switch to ring.
